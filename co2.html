    ABC & Zero-Point Calibration

    Pro-Tip on ABC Logic

    Automatic Baseline Calibration (ABC) assumes the sensor experiences outdoor ambient air (~400-420 ppm) at least once every 7 to 8 days. Disable ABC if operating inside greenhouses or crowded classrooms permanently!
